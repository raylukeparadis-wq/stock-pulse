Category Default Colors

"No data" means a stock's sector + price combination doesn't have at least 100 similar historical trades to base a reliable rating on, so instead of showing an unreliable 🟢/⚪/🔴 strength dot, the app shows no dot at all and falls back to a plain color for whichever list it's in — blue, lime, indigo, or orange. You can spot it by the missing dot, not the color itself.

Live Quote Price

Reflects whether the current price sits inside the stock's sector's single best win-rate window. Industrials' best window is $250-500 (65.5% win rate, n=226) — the highest of any price range tested for the sector. That's why NSC (~$330) shows blue; a $50 Industrials stock wouldn't.
